# Final Premium screen assets

Design: [newlastscreen — Onboarding 8](https://www.figma.com/design/vN0bXTKV7Dc1ReRYgauaNg/newlastscreen?node-id=2161-569), exported 2026-10-17.

The logo, icons, dividers, masks, banner and timer backgrounds are original Figma exports. `background.png` is only the exported gradient background layer. Text and interactive controls are rendered in HTML/CSS.

`inter-latin.woff2` is the existing Inter font from the sibling alfaanalyticsold project, distributed under the included SIL Open Font License.

Final video: user-provided `IMG_0800.MOV` (SHA-256 `53568d9aeddd62c7476edf9f3553bef17e98cd91d55372004ac3a4bbac2b50ca`). Encoded as `assets/videos/final-premium-0800.mp4`, H.264/yuv420p, 780 × 1000, CRF 18, slow preset, faststart, no audio. Crop: 1080 × 1384 at x=0/y=66, scaled with Lanczos. Duration preserved at about 10 seconds; output 1,984,739 bytes. WebP poster is taken at 0.6 seconds.

The 15-minute welcome timer starts on the final screen and retains its deadline for the tab session. After expiry, the banner switches to a neutral Premium label without resetting the countdown. Offer pricing is provided by the existing external destination.
